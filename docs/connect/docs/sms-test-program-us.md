# 8x8 SMS Test Program (US)

8x8 lets prospective customers and partners test SMS delivery to US mobile phones on the 8x8 CPaaS platform. Test messages are sent from an 8x8 number to your own mobile phone, only when you request them in your 8x8 test account.

---

## How to opt in

1. Ask your 8x8 representative for a test account.
2. From your mobile phone, text 8X8TEST to (408) 508-5705.
3. You will receive a confirmation text. Your representative then links your number to your test account.

By texting 8X8TEST to (408) 508-5705, you agree to receive test SMS messages from 8x8, sent only when you request them in your 8x8 test account. Message frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out.

---

## What you will receive

- One-time verification codes, so you can test two-factor authentication
- Account notifications about your 8x8 test account

Every message starts with "8x8 Test:". No marketing messages are sent through this program.

---

## Opting out

Reply STOP at any time. You will receive one confirmation message and no further messages. Reply START to opt back in.

---

## Help

Reply HELP, or call 8x8 support at 1-866-879-8647.
