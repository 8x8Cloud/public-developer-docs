Added a standalone MMS guide under Connect > SMS, pulling together sending, receiving, and delivery receipts for MMS into one page. The underlying `mediaUrls` field and webhook behavior were already documented across the Send SMS, Inbound SMS, and Delivery Receipts reference pages — this guide is a single starting point that links out to each, plus a summary of availability (US/Canada only) and the size/URL limits for outbound media.
