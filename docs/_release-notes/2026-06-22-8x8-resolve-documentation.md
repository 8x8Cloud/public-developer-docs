**8x8 Resolve** is now a product area on the developer portal, with a full documentation set. Resolve is a critical communications platform that helps your organisation reach the right people instantly during an incident — sending targeted alerts across SMS, email, voice, WhatsApp, and mobile push, then tracking who acknowledged and who needs help in real time.

## What you can do

- **Create and send incidents** — dispatch an alert to your entire organisation or a specific group in seconds, across multiple channels.
- **Automate workflows** — build scenarios that trigger on a schedule, branch on acknowledgement status, and escalate automatically.
- **Sync your contacts** — keep your recipient list current by connecting an HR system (Google Workspace, Microsoft Entra ID, Okta, or Workday).
- **Monitor delivery** — track delivery rates, acknowledgement rates, and response times from the dashboard.
