The six areas

  1. Six areas, one menu. Everything an admin does sits behind the left menu: Dashboard, Incidents, Scenarios, Contacts, Analytics and Settings. This tour covers each one, then walks through creating an incident.
  2. What is happening now. Active incidents show severity, the message and live delivery or response progress. AI-drafted incidents waiting for an admin to approve them sit above. AI-drafted incidents are coming soon in 8x8 Resolve.
  3. Always one click away. Create incident and Launch scenario are on every page. The menu beside them switches tenant.
  4. Every incident, live ones first. Each card shows who launched it, when, the severity, the message and how delivery or responses are going. Click a card for the full record.
  5. Saved response plans. Each scenario has exactly one trigger: an inbound message, a webhook or a schedule. Recipients are worked out when it runs.
  6. On, off, run or edit. A scenario that's switched off won't trigger. The play button launches it by hand and the pencil opens the builder.
  7. Everyone you can alert. All contacts, Integrations (8x8 directory, Entra ID, Google Workspace, Okta and Workday) and Malformed numbers. Contacts sync daily, or on demand.
  8. Where each contact came from. Source shows 8x8 Work, an HR or identity sync, CSV or Manual. Edit department, location and role at the source, such as Admin Console. Names and numbers are blurred in this guide.
  9. Fix phone numbers before go-live. Malformed numbers lists numbers Resolve couldn't normalise, usually because the country code is missing. Pick the region and click Normalize. Check this list before you rely on SMS or voice.
  10. The audit trail. Pick a date range and export PDF or CSV: who was sent what, when, and how they responded.
  11. Which channels work. Channel effectiveness compares channels side by side. Responses by department, above it, shows which teams are slow to answer.
  12. Templates and channels. Message templates with placeholders such as {{firstName}}, plus WhatsApp templates, channels and sender identities.

Create an incident

  1. Start from Create incident. It's at the top of every page. Incidents are for now: something has happened, or a planned one-off message.
  2. Title and severity. Severity drives how the alert looks on phones and on the dashboard.
  3. Pick the channels. The free beta includes 8x8 Work Mobile Push Notification, 8x8 Work Message and Email. Push and 8x8 Work Message (desktop) are separate channels, so you can choose either or both.
  4. Write the message. Choose a template from Settings, or pick No template to write a custom message.
  5. Who gets it, and how they answer. Select recipients, or add a filter by department, location or role. Ask for response with Standard gives I acknowledge and I need help. Web link responses work on every channel.
  6. Preview before you send. Preview shows what each channel gets. This is the alert in 8x8 Work Mobile, with the response buttons.
  7. The email version. The same incident as an email, with a Respond button that opens the web link.
  8. Create and send. This sends straight away, so check the recipients first. To try Resolve out, send a test incident to yourself.
  9. Send update, then Resolve. Send update adds to the incident's communications. Resolve closes it, and the record stays in analytics.
  10. Track every response. Severity, channels and message, then the recipients and a per-channel breakdown of sent, delivered and failed. Ask for a response and you also get response rates and time to respond. That's the tour. For help, open Help in the left menu and choose Contact support.
